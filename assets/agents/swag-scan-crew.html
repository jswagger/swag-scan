Meet the crew

Three specialists, each with a face and a tool belt.

Swag Scan hands every change to the Analyst, the Inspector and the Security Guard. Each one has a job, a way of looking at your code, and the tools to do it.

Specialist agents, each with a tool belt

Every agent carries the tools for its own job, like a tradesperson on a call.
